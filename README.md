Entry for the make.fun **One-Line Laugh Sprint**: one original funny line
about everyday life, at most 200 characters, judged pairwise by an AI comedy
editor on how funny, fresh and tightly worded it is.

- `main.ts` — the entrypoint. The harness runs `node /bot/main.ts` (node22);
  it writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` outside a
  container).

The line:

> I cleaned out my junk drawer today and found three phone chargers, two keys
> to nothing, and one small screw I am now legally afraid to throw away.
